# Ideas

The sticker works on its own, anywhere. These make it a little more fun. One rule: never cover the front with metal. Tap to test before you glue anything.

## Fridge Magnet

**You need:** a tag and a fridge magnet, 25 mm or bigger.

**Plain:** stick the tag on the front of the magnet.

**Inside an object** (a shell, a poker chip, a photo, a button, anything up to 1 cm thick):

1. Glue the tag's printed front to the back of the object. The tag only reads from its front.
2. Peel the plastic off the tag and press the magnet onto it.

## Clay Magnet

**You need:** a tag, a round magnet, clay, glue, and paint.

1. Shape the clay into anything at least 30 mm wide. Press a dent in the back for the magnet.
2. Dry or bake it before adding the tag or magnet. Heat kills tags and weakens magnets.
3. Glue in the magnet. Stick the tag on the front.
4. Paint over the tag. No metallic paint.

## Keychain

**You need:** a tag and a keychain.

1. Stick the tag on the keychain.
2. Leave your keys by the front door.

## Hidden

**You need:** just the tag.

Stick it under a plant pot, behind a picture frame, or inside a drawer in another room.

## 3D Printed

**You need:** a 25 mm tag and a print of Foqos's free [NFC brick and keychain](https://www.printables.com/model/1537982-foqos-nfc-brick-keychain).

1. Print it. No printer? A library, makerspace, or Craftcloud can.
2. Add the tag.
